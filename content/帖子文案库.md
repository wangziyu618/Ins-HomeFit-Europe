### 2. 10 Home Workout Mistakes
```
10 HOME WORKOUT MISTAKES KILLING YOUR GAINS ❌

1. No warm-up — 5 min dynamic stretching is non-negotiable
2. Training the same muscles every day — they need 48h recovery
3. Rushing through reps — form > speed, always
4. No progressive overload — add reps/slow tempo weekly
5. Ignoring nutrition — abs are made in the kitchen
6. Poor sleep — 7-8h is when muscle grows
7. Not tracking workouts — how do you know you're progressing?
8. Skipping leg day — yes, you can train legs at home
9. No plan — random workouts = random results
10. Giving up after 2 weeks — consistency beats intensity

Which one are you guilty of? 👇

### 3. Protein Intake Guide
```
HOW MUCH PROTEIN DO YOU ACTUALLY NEED? 🥩

Swipe for the simple guide →

Slide 1: Sedentary adult: 0.8g per kg bodyweight
Slide 2: Regular exerciser: 1.2-1.6g per kg
Slide 3: Muscle building: 1.6-2.2g per kg
Slide 4: Fat loss (preserving muscle): 1.8-2.4g per kg
Slide 5: Example: 70kg person training regularly = 84-112g/day
Slide 6: Easy sources: chicken, eggs, Greek yogurt, tofu, lentils
