### Lunge Mistakes to Avoid
```
🚫 LUNGE MISTAKES TO AVOID 🚫

STOP DOING LUNGES LIKE THIS

• Step length: not too long
• Front knee over ankle
• Chest tall, don't lean forward
• Push through front heel
• Control the descent, don't bounce

Fix these and your lunges will feel 10x better.
📌 Save for form check
```
